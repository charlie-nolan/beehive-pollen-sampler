# Bench tests (first season)

Each test has what it checks and what counts as a pass. Results go in a dated section below each test.

| # | Test | Checks | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Needle-skewer hand test | Does ~1 mm of skewer hold a pellet through a thread cut? Is the hole visible on the cut face? | Pellet stays put through the cut; cut face usable for a smear |
| 2 | Throat fit | Do 2-4 mm pellets all sit centred in the throat, or does it need a V shape? | Pellets across the size range sit centred on the gate |
| 3 | Smear quality | PG/ethanol streak vs dry tape at 400x: halos? length of single-layer region? | A single-layer region big enough to count grains, without halos |
| 4 | Clearing fluid stability | Grain swelling at day 0, 7 and 30; dry adhesive border seal on a wound spool | No meaningful swelling; seal holds on the spool |
| 5 | Blank cycles | Full cycles with no pellet | Carryover measured; target none visible on the blank frame |
| 6 | Trap on a real hive | Spring, fresh pellets | Grid deploys and retracts on schedule, fails open, colony behaviour normal |

Open questions: hive type (Langstroth 8 or 10 frame), Wi-Fi on site, stand height, frozen pellets for winter testing.
